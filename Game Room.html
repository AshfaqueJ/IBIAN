The Gilded Game Room

Where strategy meets sophistication — billiards, darts, chess, and the timeless art of gentlemanly competition.

✦ MASTER CRAFTSMANSHIP • BILLIARDS PARLOR ✦

The Art of the Cue

At the heart of The Gilded Game Room stands four immaculate regulation-sized billiards tables, each handcrafted by legendary English makers. The tables feature slate beds heated to tournament specifications, covered in championship-grade emerald baize, and framed in hand-carved mahogany. Our collection includes two American pool tables and two British snooker tables, ensuring purists of both traditions feel at home. Overhead, custom brass pendant lamps cast a warm glow without glare — the same fixtures used at the World Billiards Championship. Members often gather for friendly "Gentleman's Doubles" on Thursday evenings, followed by single-malt tastings from the adjacent humidor lounge.

“The click of ivory on ivory, the hush before a brilliant bank shot — this is where deals are sealed and friendships forged.” — longtime member, Harold P.

Complimentary cues of English ash and American maple are available from the concierge, alongside house-chalked tips and bridge sticks. Private billiards lessons with a national champion can be arranged by request.

✦ STRATEGY & PRECISION • DARTS & BOARD GAMES ✦

Where Minds & Hands Align

Beyond the billiards hall lies the Salon of Strategy — home to three professional-grade dart lanes with bristle boards and official Winmau blade surfaces, calibrated to PDC standards. A dedicated chess corner features six hand-carved rosewood and boxwood sets on marble-topped tables, inviting both casual blitz and hours-long classical matches. For those who prefer cards, a separate card room offers bridge, poker, and whist tables with leather armchairs and green felt surfaces. The game room also houses backgammon, mahjong, and a 19th-century style shuffleboard table restored from an English country manor. The atmosphere is hushed and warm — punctuated only by the soft thud of a dart striking the board or the quiet satisfaction of a checkmate.

“Every Tuesday, our Chess Master holds an open simul. Beginners are welcomed as warmly as grandmasters.”

A dedicated game concierge can arrange tournaments, private lessons, or simply explain the rules of cricket darts. The room is open until midnight, with a butler service delivering coffee, cigars, and light refreshments.

✦ The Parlor Distinctions ✦

Classic games, impeccable settings, and timeless traditions

Billiards & Snooker

Four championship tables (2 pool, 2 snooker). Custom English cues, heated slate, and weekly "Silk & Cue" tournaments. House pro available.

Professional Darts

Three Winmau Blade 6 boards, tungsten darts provided. Electronic scoreboards and regulation oche. Monthly doubles league.

Chess & Strategy

Six rosewood sets, marble boards. Grandmaster-led sessions each Tuesday. Chess clocks available for timed matches.

Card Room

Three dedicated tables for bridge, poker, whist. Humidity-controlled card storage. Private dealer request available.

Backgammon & Mahjong

Handcrafted inlaid backgammon sets, Chinese mahjong tiles (Hong Kong style). Weekly social play on Saturdays.

Humidor & Lounge

Adjacent to the game room: a climate-controlled walk-in humidor and a bar serving vintage port, cognac, and single malts.

Open daily 11 AM – 12 AM Professional instructors on call Annual Gilded Games Championship (May) Private lockers for equipment Dedicated butler service
