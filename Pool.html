Aqua Aurora

The Crown Jewel of IBIAN — an infinity pool where Los Angeles meets the horizon, and every swim feels like a escape into serenity.

✦ INFINITY EDGE • PANORAMIC VISTAS ✦

Where Water Meets Sky

Suspended on the western terrace of the IBIAN Club, the Aqua Aurora infinity pool offers an unparalleled aquatic experience. Its vanishing edge blends seamlessly with the California skyline, creating an illusion of endless water stretching toward the Pacific. Heated to a perfect 84°F year-round, the pool is flanked by private cabanas draped in pearl-white linen, each equipped with chilled towel service and a dedicated attendant. Whether you seek morning laps as the sun rises over the hills or twilight lounging beneath fibre-optic starlight, Aqua Aurora is a sanctuary for the senses.

“The water shimmers like liquid sapphire at dusk, and the city lights twinkle below — a perspective reserved exclusively for IBIAN members.”

The pool deck is crafted from Portuguese limestone and heated natural stone pathways, ensuring comfort even on cooler evenings. Underwater speakers play soft ambient scores, while the adjacent Aqua Bar serves handcrafted elixirs, from cucumber mint coolers to vintage rosé. It's not merely a pool — it's a lifestyle statement.

✦ CONCIERGE WELLNESS • PRIVATE HYDROTHERAPY ✦

Beyond the Lap Lane

Aquatic wellness reaches new heights at Aqua Aurora. The facility includes a separate hydrotherapy pool with massaging jets, a cold plunge at 52°F for recovery, and an infrared sauna overlooking the terrace. Certified instructors offer private aqua yoga sessions at dawn, as well as guided swim clinics for all levels. For those seeking pure relaxation, the heated loungers and misting system transform the deck into a year-round resort destination.

Every weekend, the pool hosts "Champagne Floats" — a curated tasting of grower champagnes served on floating trays. Members frequently gather for the celebrated Equinox Swim, a meditative event timed with sunrise. The poolside changing suites feature rainfall showers, organic bath amenities, and plush robes by a legendary Italian atelier. More than a fitness amenity, Aqua Aurora is a social nexus where business magnates, artists, and innovators unwind in hushed elegance.

Exclusive access: open daily from 6 AM to 11 PM. Private events and cabana reservations available via the club concierge.
