The Ambience

Chef Kazi Anwar

CULINARY DIRECTOR | MASTER OF BENGALI GASTRONOMY

Hailing from the historic culinary traditions of Dhaka, Chef Kazi Anwar brings the soul of Bangladesh to The IBIAN Club. With over 18 years of experience in Michelin-starred kitchens across London, Dubai, and Dhaka, Chef Anwar masterfully weaves the intricate spices, slow-cooked delicacies, and forgotten recipes of his homeland into a contemporary fine-dining tapestry.

His philosophy honors the "Mughal-Bengali" fusion — fragrant biryanis, mustard-infused seafood, and caramelized sweets — while embracing modern plating and seasonal ingredients.

🍛 Signature: Royal Ilish Pulao & Mango Mustard Glaze
