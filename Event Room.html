Moments That Define

Step into a world of legendary galas, intimate salons, and unforgettable celebrations — where every event becomes a cherished memory.

✦ THE CROWN JEWEL • ANNUAL MASQUERADE ✦

The Enchanted Masquerade

Each December, on the second Saturday before Christmas, IBIAN Club transforms into a Venetian dreamscape for our legendary Masquerade Gala. Members don handcrafted masks and black-tie attire, descending the grand staircase into the Cathedral Ballroom, where live string quartets give way to a twelve-piece orchestra. The 2024 edition (December 14th) featured a cascading chandelier of 5,000 crystals and a surprise performance by a Grammy-winning vocalist. Guests danced until 3 AM, sipping vintage champagne beneath a ceiling of floating candles. One attendee called it “the night time stood still — pure magic, from the first waltz to the last toast.”

“As the clock struck midnight, the room erupted in confetti of gold leaf. It was not just a party — it was a declaration of joy.” — longtime member, Eleanor V.

The Masquerade has become the most coveted invitation in the city, with waiting lists opening in June. Members cherish the tradition of the “Reveal” — unmasking together just before the final waltz, symbolizing authenticity and connection. This is the essence of IBIAN: celebration with soul.

Next edition: December 13, 2025 | Invitations dispatched in October
✦ EPICUREAN EXCELLENCE • CHEF'S TABLE SERIES ✦

Gastronomic Journeys

Our quarterly Chef's Table dinners have become the stuff of legend. Past editions have seen Michelin-starred icons from Paris, Tokyo, and Lima take over the Gilded Kitchen, crafting twelve-course tasting menus paired with rare vintages from the club's 3,000-bottle cellar. In spring 2025 (March 21st), Chef Amara Okonkwo created a “Silk Road” feast — saffron-infused broths, dry-aged wagyu, and a dessert course lit by edible gold leaf. Members describe these evenings as “transformative,” where strangers become friends over shared wonder. Each dinner is limited to twenty-four seats, ensuring intimacy and conversation that flows until late into the night.

“I’ve attended dinners across the world, but nothing compares to the warmth and artistry of IBIAN’s Chef's Table. It’s why I became a member.” — James L., Founding Patron

Beyond formal dinners, IBIAN hosts monthly “Culinary Salons” — intimate cooking demonstrations, wine workshops, and meet-the-maker evenings with artisanal producers. These gatherings celebrate the club's belief that food is not mere sustenance, but a bridge between cultures and hearts.

Next dinner: June 19, 2025 (Summer Solstice Edition) | Booking opens May 1st

✦ Legacy in Motion ✦

Iconic celebrations from our recent archive — relive the magic

Champagne & Sonata

October 25, 2024 | 7:30 PM

An evening with the Philharmonic's first chair, followed by a vertical tasting of Krug vintages (1988, 1996, 2008). Members toasted under the stars on the Terrace Royale until midnight. The night raised $47,000 for the IBIAN Music Scholarship Fund.

Curator’s Salon

September 12, 2024 | 6:00 PM

Private viewing of the Rothschild collection, led by the museum's director. Followed by a discussion on art as legacy — a sellout event with 80 attendees. The evening concluded with a champagne reception in the Sculpture Garden until 10 PM.

Jazz at Midnight

November 8 & 9, 2024 | 10:00 PM – 2:00 AM

Speakeasy-style evenings featuring Grammy-nominated artist Samara Joy. Previous performers include Diana Krall (March 2024) and Jon Batiste (September 2023), with after-parties in the Cellar Lounge. Each session limited to 120 guests.

Global Leaders Forum

February 28, 2025 | 4:00 PM – 8:00 PM

Intimate dialogues with Nobel laureate Dr. Sarah Al-Mansour and former UN Ambassador Kenneth Cole in the Library Rotunda. Followed by a private dinner with 30 members. The 2024 edition featured a conversation on ethical AI with Sundar Pichai.

Harvest Moon Gala

September 29, 2024 | 6:30 PM – 11:00 PM

Annual farm-to-table feast held on the rooftop garden. Live folk music, artisanal cocktails from local distilleries, and a silent auction benefiting the IBIAN Foundation's environmental grants. Raised $62,000 for urban farming initiatives.

New Year's Spectacular

December 31, 2024 – January 1, 2025 | 9:00 PM – 3:00 AM

360-degree views of the city fireworks, a live countdown from the Grand Balcony, and breakfast (truffle scrambled eggs & caviar) served at 2 AM. The most anticipated ticket of the season, sold out in 48 hours. Champagne tower featured 600 glasses of Dom Pérignon 2015.

“I joined for the pool — I stayed for the galas. Unforgettable.” “Every event feels like a family reunion, if your family had world-class champagne.” “The Chef's Table alone is worth the membership.” “December Masquerade 2024 changed my definition of celebration.”
