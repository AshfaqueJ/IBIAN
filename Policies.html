The Covenant of Excellence

Rules, policies, and membership requirements that preserve the spirit, integrity, and distinction of the IBIAN Club.

General Conduct & Etiquette

Preserving an atmosphere of mutual respect and refinement
  • Respect for All Members and Staff: Every individual within the club shall be treated with dignity, courtesy, and discretion. Harassment, discrimination, or offensive language of any kind is grounds for immediate suspension.
  • Dress Code: Business attire or elevated casual is required in all common areas. Jackets are recommended for the Main Dining Room after 6 PM. Athletic wear is permitted only in designated fitness zones.
  • Mobile Device Policy: Phone calls are prohibited in the Library, Billiards Room, and Main Lounge. Devices must be set to silent. Photography of other members without explicit consent is forbidden.
  • Guest Privileges: Members may sponsor guests, subject to a daily guest fee. The sponsoring member remains fully responsible for the guest's conduct. No unaccompanied guests permitted.
  • Alcohol & Substance Policy: The club serves alcohol responsibly. Intoxication that disturbs others will result in immediate removal and potential membership review. Illegal substances are strictly prohibited.

Facility Usage & Reservations

Ensuring fair access and preservation of club assets

Reservation Protocol

Dining tables, private event spaces, and sports facilities may be reserved up to 14 days in advance via the member portal or concierge desk. Cancellations require at least 4 hours' notice; repeated no-shows incur a fee and may affect reservation privileges.

Damage & Liability

Members are financially responsible for any damage caused by themselves or their guests to club property, artwork, or furnishings. The club is not liable for personal property lost or stolen on premises.

Hours of Operation

The main clubhouse operates daily from 7:00 AM to 1:00 AM. Select amenities (fitness center, 24-hour library) remain accessible via keycard. Overnight stays are not permitted unless pre-arranged for events.

Membership Requirements

Standards for admission and continued fellowship
  • Minimum age: 25 years
  • Two active members in good standing as sponsors
  • Completed application & background review
  • Interview with Membership Committee
  • Initiation fee (varies by membership tier)
  • Monthly dues + annual capital assessment

Financial Obligations & Code of Honor

Commitment to the club's enduring legacy
  • Dues & Fees: Monthly dues are billed on the 1st of each month and payable by the 15th. Late payments incur a 5% penalty. Delinquency beyond 60 days results in suspension of privileges and potential termination.
  • Confidentiality: Members shall not disclose any private conversations, member lists, or internal club matters without express permission. The IBIAN Club values discretion above all.
  • Conflict of Interest: No member shall use the club for solicitation, commercial enterprise, or political campaigning without prior written approval from the Board of Governors.
  • Resignation & Termination: Resignations require 30 days' written notice. The Board reserves the right to terminate membership for violation of these covenants, with a two-thirds majority vote.

Membership Classes & Privileges

Tailored tiers for diverse lifestyles

Full Resident Membership

Unlimited access to all facilities, dining, events, and reciprocal clubs worldwide. Includes voting rights and eligibility for Board positions.

Social Membership

Access to dining rooms, bars, and social events. Excludes fitness center and billiards room. No voting rights.

Non-Resident Membership

For members residing outside a 50-mile radius. Full privileges but limited to 20 visits per calendar year.

Legacy Membership

Available to children of current members (ages 25–35). Reduced initiation fee and full privileges for first five years.

Enforcement & Governing Body

Maintaining accountability and fairness
  • Board of Governors: An elected body of nine members oversees all policy enforcement, disciplinary actions, and charter amendments. Board meetings are held quarterly.
  • Grievance Procedure: Any member may file a written complaint with the General Manager. The Governance Committee will review and recommend action within 30 days.
  • Amendments: These rules may be amended by a two-thirds vote of the Board of Governors, with 30 days' notice to the membership. Proposed amendments are posted in the library and member portal.

The complete Charter & Bylaws are available for review in the club's main office and secure member portal.
