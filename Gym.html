Aurora Athletica

The pinnacle of private fitness — where biomechanics meets bespoke luxury, and every workout is a masterclass in performance.

✦ STATE OF THE ART • BIOMECHANICS LAB ✦

Strength. Science. Serenity.

Aurora Athletica isn't simply a gym — it's a sanctuary for human optimization. Spanning 8,000 square feet of curated fitness space, the facility houses the latest generation of Technogym Artis equipment, each piece crafted from sustainable materials and integrated with AI-driven performance tracking. From Kinesis walls to anti-gravity treadmills, every machine is calibrated to reduce joint impact while maximizing output. Floor-to-ceiling electrochromic glass adjusts tint throughout the day, offering privacy during sunrise sessions or panoramic city views as the sun sets.

“Where elite training meets holistic recovery — Aurora Athletica has redefined what a members' gym can be.”

A dedicated functional training zone features monkey bars, climbing ropes, and a half-Olympic lifting platform with Eleiko competition plates. Personal trainers — all holding advanced degrees in exercise science — offer bespoke programming, from metabolic conditioning to longevity-focused mobility work. The space is acoustically treated, with soft ambient lighting and a curated wellness soundtrack. After your session, step into the cold plunge or the Himalayan salt sauna.

✦ RECOVERY SUITE • BIOHACKING HUB ✦

Beyond the Rep

True fitness is built on recovery, and Aurora Athletica's wellness wing is second to none. The Recovery Lounge includes a cryotherapy chamber, Normatec compression boots, and a zero-gravity massage chair overlooking the courtyard. Hyperbaric oxygen therapy and red light therapy panels are available for members seeking accelerated muscle repair. For those focused on data, the on-site biohacking station offers VO₂ max testing, resting metabolic rate analysis, and DEXA body composition scans — all interpreted by certified physiologists.

The facility also houses a boutique fitness studio that hosts daily classes: sunrise Vinyasa yoga, high-intensity interval training, and Pilates reformer sessions limited to six participants. Post-workout, the Vitality Bar serves cold-pressed tonics, adaptogen lattes, and protein blends crafted by a Michelin-trained nutritionist. Members also enjoy direct access to the club's sports medicine concierge, offering physical therapy and recovery protocols tailored to individual goals.

Open 5 AM – midnight, 365 days a year. Personal training and recovery consultations available by reservation.
