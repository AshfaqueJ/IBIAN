Allies of Distinction

The esteemed partners, collaborators, and patrons who help elevate the IBIAN experience — from luxury brands to cultural institutions.

A Network of Excellence

The IBIAN Club is honored to collaborate with world-renowned organizations that share our commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and cultural enrichment. These partnerships bring exclusive benefits, unique experiences, and unparalleled access to our members.

✧ Luxury & Lifestyle ✧

Maison Delamotte

Official Champagne House

Since 2020, Maison Delamotte has been the exclusive champagne provider for all IBIAN galas and private events. Members receive priority access to vineyard tours and curated tastings at the Reims estate.

Bespoke Auto

Mobility Concierge

A curated fleet of Rolls-Royce, Bentley, and Aston Martin vehicles available for member travel. Complimentary chauffeured service for arrivals to club events.

Graff Diamonds

Exclusive Jewelry Partner

Private viewings of high jewelry collections at the club twice annually. Members receive dedicated consultation services and invitations to international trunk shows.

Ritz-Carlton Yacht Collection

Preferred Cruise Partner

Exclusive member rates and onboard credits for luxury Mediterranean and Caribbean voyages. Priority booking for IBIAN-sponsored group cruises.

✧ Culinary & Spirits ✧

The Macallan

Single Malt Partner

Exclusive single cask bottlings available only at IBIAN. Members enjoy priority access to distillery experiences and biennial whisky dinners with master distillers.

Gaggenau

Kitchen Appliance Partner

Sponsors the Chef's Table Series and provides culinary demonstrations using state-of-the-art appliances. Members receive design consultations for private residences.

Belvedere Vodka

Premium Spirits Partner

Signature cocktail creations for member bars. Exclusive tastings and bespoke bottling events hosted quarterly in the Cellar Lounge.

Illycaffè

Coffee Concierge

Artisanal coffee program across all club lounges. Members receive monthly bean deliveries and invitations to master barista classes.

✧ Cultural & Arts ✧

Los Angeles Philharmonic

Performing Arts Partner

Private pre-concert receptions, backstage meet-and-greets, and priority box seating. IBIAN members receive a 15% discount on subscription series.

Getty Foundation

Arts Benefactor Partner

Quarterly curator-led tours of special exhibitions. Collaborative sponsorship of the annual IBIAN Art Salon featuring emerging artists.

Rizzoli Bookstore

Literary Partner

Curates the IBIAN Library collection. Hosts author evenings and signed first-edition releases exclusively for members.

Leica Camera

Visual Arts Partner

Equipment loaner program for members. Annual photography exhibition in the East Wing with purchase opportunities.

✦ Premier Global Alliance ✦

The Royal Portfolio of Private Clubs — IBIAN is proud to be a founding member of this distinguished collection of 27 private clubs across 14 countries. Members enjoy reciprocal access to sister clubs in London, Hong Kong, Paris, Dubai, and New York, including full dining, event, and guest privileges. This alliance represents the pinnacle of global hospitality and discretion.

No daily guest fees at reciprocal clubs Priority room bookings at partner hotels Global events calendar

✧ Wellness & Appearance ✧

La Mer

Skincare Partner

Exclusive spa treatments at the IBIAN Wellness Center. Members receive a 20% discount on products and private consultations with estheticians.

Truefitt & Hill

Barber & Grooming

On-site barbershop operating Wednesdays and Saturdays. Royal Warrant holders since 1805. Members enjoy preferential booking.

Equinox Hotels

Fitness & Hospitality

Cross-access to Equinox fitness clubs nationwide. Members receive complimentary guest passes for traveling companions.

Patek Philippe

Timepiece Partner

Exclusive viewing salons and priority allocation of limited editions. Annual watchmaking symposium held at the club.
